Atractions

Being a village that is primarily focused on production there aren't too many tourist attractions, but there are a few. The beauty of the village is a major highlight, despite the village being characterized by fire and iron, the village is very colourful. Located to the far right of the steelworks is the Buddy Plaza, a scenic area where hunting buddies such as palicos (Felynes) and palamutes (doglike creatures) are trained for hunting.

One of the prettiest locations is the Gathering Hub, a gathering area for hunters looking to take quests from the Hunters Guild, an organization dedicated to protecting the ecosystem from damage. The gathering hub is a unique building. In the centre is a beautiful cherry tree, and past that is a balcony by the river with multiple waterfalls adorning the scenery.
