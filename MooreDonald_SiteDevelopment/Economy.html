Economy

Kamura has quite a few services to offer. The most important of these, is its massive steelworks, where Kumura's known specialty Tatara steel, is used to create massive weapons and strong armour, as well as other things. In front of the massive steelworks, there are multiple stalls, with vendors selling all kinds of products such as roasted chestnuts and other delicacies, vegetables, hunting supplies, and more. Also by the steelworks is a blacksmith shop, run by Hamon the blacksmith. He is always ready to provide monster hunters expertly crafted armour and weapons.

To the left of the steelworks is Yomogi’s tea shop, a restaurant that sells tea and Bunny Dango, a specialty so good that it has started to be exported to other lands, most notably Elgado Outpost (another location in monster hunter).
