About Kamura Village

The Village of Kamura is a location in the Monster Hunter series of games, specifically Monster Hunter Rise. The village is known for its impressive steelwork, and delectable Bunny Dango (Dango are Japanese sweet dumplings made from regular rice flour and glutinous rice flour, I don't know why it has bunny in its name).

The Kamura standing today is the second Kamura village, the first was destroyed in one terrifying night when a horde of monsters went berserk and attacked the settlement. This became known as The Rampage. It is a tight-knit community, treating each other like family, but they're welcoming to visitors.
