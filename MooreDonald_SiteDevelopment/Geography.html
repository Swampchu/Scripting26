Geography

The geography is truly stunning. The village is nestled in the mountains, located on an island in a large river, surrounded by forests. The village itself is very picturesque, looking like an old Japanese... well village, with a giant building known as the steelworks located in the centre.

A short distance away from the village is a giant canyon, the road through the canyon is torn and battered, likely from multiple monster attacks. In this canyon, there's a MASSIVE stronghold, ready to prepare for any attacks or rampages from monsters. The stronghold has everything that you would need to repel a rampage; arrow turrets and cannons to repel monsters, Dragonators (ridiculously huge spears) that poke out of the ground, and a splitting wyvern shot - a giant launchable shell that causes an even greater explosion.
